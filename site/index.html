WHO WE ARE

A layout-first semiconductor design partner.

We work alongside fabless semiconductor companies and design teams as an extension of their layout and physical design function — engaged on individual blocks, full chips, or ongoing partnerships.

WHAT WE DO

Services overview

From transistor-level analog layout to full RTL-to-GDSII delivery. Explore the complete list on the Services page.

01 · Layout

Analog & Custom Layout

Transistor-level layout for analog, mixed-signal, and custom-digital blocks, optimized for matching, parasitics, and yield.

02 · Physical Design

Physical Design & RTL-to-GDSII

Floorplanning, placement, CTS, routing, and signoff — taking a design from netlist to a tape-out-ready database.

03 · Quality

DFT & Verification

Design-for-test insertion and physical/functional verification to catch issues before they reach silicon.
